# Southbound Gear — early concept (not approved for implementation)

Status: idea / exploration only. No store, advertising, links or changes to the public travel app without explicit approval.

## Vision
A durable, high-quality outdoor and backpacking label blending technical function (Patagonia-like reliability as an aspiration, not an affiliation) with contemporary travel/streetwear design. Long-term assortment could include backpacks, camping gear, outerwear, clothing and footwear; avoid launching all categories at once.

## Visual identity
Working name: **Southbound**; abbreviated stacked monogram:

    ─────
     STH
     BND
    ─────

Upper rule above STH, lower rule below BND. Minimal geometric typography; the generated first visual is an exploration, not a finalized or cleared trademark. Logo artwork has not been added to the app.

## Initial product hypothesis
**High-quality rain jacket** as first potential piece, balancing outdoor functionality and everyday travel aesthetics. Before committing: define use case, waterproofness/breathability, seam taping, fit, weight, packability, repairability, durable trims, material sourcing, product safety, supplier quality control and testing. Prototype and test on real trips; assess warranty/returns, MOQ, cash flow and pricing. Avoid unsupported durability or sustainability claims.

## Relationship to travel app
Southbound Travel remains useful and independent. Potential future community-driven product feedback, testing and optional non-intrusive brand link only with explicit permission; never misrepresent editorial travel recommendations as independent if commercially influenced.

## Preliminary name-risk scan (2026-10-12)
**Not cleared.** US SOUTHBOUND word mark registration no. 3944227, class 25, includes jackets, coats, footwear; publicly listed as registered/renewed: https://trademarks.justia.com/851/08/southbound-85108298.html . Separate SOUTHBOUND registrations for apparel have been reported in China: https://tm.aliyun.com/detail/20b4_15967326_25 and https://tm.aliyun.com/detail/d966_8944814_25 . These are jurisdiction-specific, not proof of a German/EU conflict or freedom to operate. Owner information on third-party directories may be stale; verify with official registers.

**Mandatory before use:** Search DPMAregister, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database and relevant common-law/commercial names for SOUTHBOUND, STH/BND and confusingly similar variants; assess Nice classes 25 (clothing), 18 (bags), 22 (tents) and 35 (retail), as relevant. Ask a trademark professional for clearance of Germany/EU and planned markets. EUIPO search guidance: https://www.euipo.europa.eu/de/trade-marks/before-applying/availability . Check domains and social handles separately; availability is not trademark clearance.

## Validation steps
1. Complete trademark clearance before investment in naming, stock or promotion.
2. Interview target backpackers and identify an underserved rain-jacket need.
3. Produce a technical specification, prototypes and field-test plan.
4. Calculate landed cost, MOQ, defect/return reserve and realistic unit economics.
5. Test demand through a nonbinding waitlist; only then decide whether to produce.

**Decision:** This is documented only; not an instruction to build an e-commerce feature or divert the ongoing travel-app budget audit.
